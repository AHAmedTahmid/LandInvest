1. Welcome to LandInvest

LandInvest is a smart accounting & management system made for land-investment partnerships (called syndicates) in Bangladesh.

What LandInvest does (in simple words)

Running a land partnership means many people invest together to buy land, build on it, sell plots and share the profit.

LandInvest keeps everything in one place:

  • Partners — the people who invested money into the partnership.
  • Land projects — the plots of land the partnership owns or is building on.
  • Sales — selling plots to buyers, and tracking installments (monthly payments).
  • Expenses — recording money spent on buying land, development, labor, etc.
  • Treasury — tracking money received (from sales) and money paid (for expenses / partner withdrawals).
  • Profit distribution — splitting the net profit among partners according to their share.
  • Reports — creating financial reports and exporting them as PDF or Excel.

How the money flows (simple diagram)

1. Partner invests capital -> 2. Syndicate buys land / develops plots -> 3. Syndicate sells plot to buyer -> 4. Buyer pays installments -> 5. Money received & paid out (Treasury) -> 6. Revenue - Costs = Profit -> 7. Profit split among partners 
      [Partner A] --invests--> [Syndicate Land Fund]
        --> [Buy Land / Build] --> [Sell Plots] --> [Buyer Inst. Payments]
        --> [Treasury: Money In / Money Out]
        --> [Net Profit] --split by share--> [Partner A, Partner B, ...]
In simple terms: LandInvest is the single source of truth for money, land, sales and profit of your partnership.

2. Getting Started

This manual helps you use LandInvest step by step. You need:

  • An internet browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
  • The LandInvest web address: http://localhost:3000
  • Your login email and password (provided by your administrator).

2.1 System access

The application is already installed and running on the server. Ask your administrator for the correct web address and login details.

2.2 First login

  1. Open your browser and go to the LandInvest address.
  2. On the login page, type your registered email address.
  3. Type your password.
  4. Click the Sign In button.
  5. If the details are correct, you will be taken to the Dashboard.
Tip: If you forgot your password, use the Forgot Password option on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

3. Logging In / Logging Out / Account

3.1 Logout

  1. Click your name / profile icon in the top-right corner.
  2. Click Logout.
  3. The login page will appear again.

3.2 Change password

  1. Go to Settings (or your profile menu).
  2. Open Change Password.
  3. Enter current password, new password and confirm new password.
  4. Click Save.

3.3 Inviting a new user

  1. Go to Settings > Users & Invites (depending on your role permissions).
  2. Click Invite User.
  3. Enter the email, full name and assign a role.
  4. Send the invitation. The invited person receives an email with sign-up instructions.

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your first screen after login. It gives you a quick summary:

  • Total partners / capital.
  • Active land projects.
  • Total sales and collections.
  • Current expenses and treasury balance.
  • Profit summary.

How to use it

  1. Log in as usual.
  2. You land on Dashboard.
  3. Use the menu on the left / top to jump to any module.
The Dashboard refreshes automatically. You do not need to refresh manually.

5. Partners

Partners are the people or entities who put money into the partnership (partner capital).

What you can do

  • Add a new partner.
  • View partner details (capital contributed, expenses, sales, profit, withdrawals).
  • See a summary of all partners.

How to add a partner

  1. Open the Partners menu.
  2. Click Add Partner (or the + button).
  3. Fill in the partner information (name, capital, share, etc.).
  4. Click Save.
  5. The partner appears in the partner list.
Important: Adding a partner does not mean money has come in yet. Partner capital is usually created when the partner contributes to the partnership fund.

6. Sales & Buyers

This module handles plot sales: which plot was sold, to which buyer, at what price, and through which installments.

Key screens

  • Sales Register — list of all sales (buyer, price, paid amount, due amount).
  • Plot Inventory — plots by status with inventory value.
  • Buyer Statement — contract details, installments, payments, remaining due.
  • Installment Due / Overdue — upcoming and overdue installments with buyer phone.
  • Collection Report — payments received in a period.

How to record / follow a sale

  1. Open the Sales menu.
  2. Open the relevant Sale / Project.
  3. Enter or update buyer and plot details.
  4. Record installment payments against the sale.
  5. Use the Reports > Sales Register to see status: Paid / Due / Overdue.

7. Expenses

Expenses are money paid out by the partnership (land acquisition, development, labor, misc).

How to record an expense

  1. Open the Expenses menu.
  2. Click New Expense.
  3. Select the Project, category (Registration, Mutation, Bayna, Labor, Survey, Misc), amount, date, payee, etc.
  4. Save the expense.

Approval

Some expenses may need approval. Check the Pending Approval report to see expenses waiting for approval and their age in days.

8. Vouchers

Every financial transaction in LandInvest that involves money is recorded through a voucher.

Where vouchers are generated

  • Expense Vouchers — generated automatically when a new expense is recorded (land acquisition, development, labor, survey, etc.) from the Expenses module.
  • Payment Vouchers — generated when a payment is made out of the partnership funds.
  • Petty Cash Vouchers — generated for small cash expenses (petty cash).

What a voucher contains

  • Voucher number (auto-generated from the expense / transaction reference).
  • Date of the transaction.
  • Receiver / payee name and phone.
  • Amount and purpose of payment.
  • Payment mode (cash / bank / partner), account head, cheque number.

Attached voucher image

  1. When recording an expense, attach the scanned voucher / bill / receipt image using the voucher file field.
  2. The image is stored together with the expense record.

Where to see vouchers

  1. Go to Reports > Expense Register.
  2. This report lists all vouchers with subtotals grouped by category.

9. Projects

Land projects group plots, costs and sales together.

  • Project Cost Statement — acquisition + development expenses, cost per plot (Shotok).
  • Project Profit & Loss — invoiced and realised profit.
  • All Projects Comparison — area, cost, sales, collected, receivable, profit, ROI, status.
  • Land Document Register — Mouza, JL, Dag, Khatian numbers, area, status.
Open a project from the Projects menu to see its plots, costs, sales and reports.

10. Treasury & Money Movement

The Treasury module tracks all money: what came in and what went out.

Key screens

  • Cash Book — date-wise cash in/out with running balance.
  • Bank Book — bank account transactions with running balance.
  • Receipts & Payments Statement — money in/out grouped by purpose.
  • Contra / Fund Transfer Register — all cash/bank transfers.

How money moves

Money In (Sale installments / capital) --> Treasury
Money Out (Expenses / payouts) --> Treasury
Balance = Money In - Money Out

11. Reports

LandInvest provides a rich set of reports. All reports can be exported as PDF or Excel.

Report categories

How to open and use a report

  1. Open the Reports menu.
  2. Choose a report (for example Cash Book).
  3. Apply filters if needed (date range, project, partner, status, sale, user, table, action).
  4. Click Generate / Run.
  5. To export, use the PDF or Excel export button.
The exported file (PDF / Excel) can be shared with clients, auditors or management for review.

12. Profit Distribution

After sales generate revenue and costs are recorded, the net profit is distributed among partners according to their capital share.

Steps

  1. Check the Profit Allocation Sheet report.
  2. Check Profit Distribution History to see past runs.
  3. Confirm amounts with the partnership owner before distributing to partners.
[Sales Revenue] - [Project Costs] = [Net Profit] | | split by Partner Share (%) v [Partner A] [Partner B] [Partner C]

13. Users, Roles, Audit & Billing

Roles

Each user gets a role (Owner, Managing Partner, Site Manager, Accountant, Auditor, Sales Agent, etc.) which controls what they can see and do. Permissions are managed from Settings.

Audit Trail

The system keeps an Audit Trail of who changed what and when, plus User Activity.

Billing

Syndicate subscription / billing records are under Billing.

16. Support

If you need help, contact your system administrator or the LandInvest support team.

For technical issues related to server setup, contact your administrator. For usage questions, your manager / partner owner can assist.

Glossary (simple terms)

  • Partner — person who invested in the partnership.
  • Plot — a piece of land.
  • Buyer — person who buys the plot.
  • Installment — scheduled payment by buyer.
  • Expense — money spent by the partnership.
  • Profit — money left after selling land minus costs.
  • Capital — money contributed by partners.
